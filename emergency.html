Treat These As Urgent

Call immediately if you notice

Burning smell or sparks

A fishy or burning smell near plugs or the DB board, buzzing sockets, or visible sparking. Switch off at the mains if safe to do so, then call us.

Board won't reset

Earth leakage or main breaker trips instantly every time you flip it back. Something downstream is faulty, and forcing it is dangerous.

Partial or total power loss

Your house is dark while the neighbours have power, or only some rooms are dead. Often a connection fault that gets worse the longer it's left.

FAQ

Emergency call-out questions

Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

Yes. We're a genuine 24/7 operation. The same number works at 02:00 on a Sunday as it does on a weekday morning. If a call slips through while we're on a job, we phone back as soon as hands are free.

What does an after-hours call-out cost?

After-hours call-outs carry a standard call-out fee, quoted to you on the phone before we dispatch. The repair itself is quoted separately on site, and no chargeable work starts without your go-ahead.

What should I do while I wait?

If there's smoke, sparking or a burning smell, switch off the main breaker at your DB board if it's safe to reach. Don't touch damaged cables or wet electrical equipment, and keep people and pets clear of the affected area.

Which areas do you cover after hours?

Somerset West, Strand, Gordon's Bay and the greater Helderberg. If you're just outside these areas, call anyway. If we can get to you quickly, we will.
