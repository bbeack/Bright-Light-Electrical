What Is A COC?

The certificate every property transfer needs

An Electrical Certificate of Compliance (COC) is a legal document confirming your electrical installation meets the SANS 10142 wiring standard. In South Africa you cannot transfer a property without a valid one, and insurers can repudiate claims where an installation was non-compliant.

A COC is valid for two years for transfer purposes, but any new electrical work done after it's issued must also be certified. We inspect honestly, fix only what's genuinely required, and issue the certificate without dragging the process out.

FAQ

COC questions, answered

How long does a COC inspection take?

A standard home takes roughly 1 to 2 hours to inspect. If the installation passes, the certificate can be issued the same day. If remedial work is needed, the timeline depends on the scope, and we quote it before anything starts.

How much does a COC cost?

The inspection is a fixed fee that we confirm when you book. Remedial work, if any is required, is quoted separately and itemised so you can see exactly what's needed for compliance and why.

My sale is urgent. How fast can you move?

Tell us the transfer deadline when you call. Urgent inspections can often be done within 24 hours, and we prioritise remedial work on transfer properties so the certificate isn't the thing holding up your sale.

Does a COC cover my solar or inverter system?

Backup power systems need their own certification. If we installed your system, that paperwork is included. If someone else installed it, we can inspect and certify it as part of the visit. Just mention it when booking.
